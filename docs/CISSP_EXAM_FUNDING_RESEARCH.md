# CISSP Exam Funding Research

Last updated: 2026-03-30

Target: CISSP exam fee ($749) and/or training covered. Also applicable to CCSP, CISM, and other advanced security certs.

Profile: Atlanta GA, college senior graduating May 2026, holds SecurityX/CASP+, SSCP, CCNA, Security+. Black male. NOT going through WIOA (too slow).

---

## TIER 1: HIGHEST PROBABILITY (Apply immediately)

### 1. ISC2 Center for Cyber Safety and Education -- Amentum CISSP Pathway Scholarship
- **What:** Free CISSP exam voucher + official textbook + study guide + practice tests + ISC2 self-paced training + first AMF paid upon certification
- **Award:** 5 scholarships per year (3 US, 1 UK, 1 international)
- **Eligibility:** Aged 18+, demonstrate financial need, must have cybersecurity experience to qualify for ISC2 membership
- **Application:** Submit academic CV, LinkedIn profile, 3 short essays
- **Deadline:** Historically opens July, deadline mid-August (2025 deadline was August 19, 2025). Watch for 2026 cycle.
- **URL:** https://www.isc2.org/Insights/2025/07/Center-for-Cyber-Safety-and-Education-and-Amentum-Scholarships
- **Your edge:** SSCP holder (already ISC2 member), 4 certs, graduating senior, financial need is real. Strong candidate.

### 2. ISC2 Center Academic Scholarships (Undergraduate)
- **What:** $1,000 to $5,000 cash scholarships for cybersecurity students
- **Award:** Up to 20 undergraduate scholarships per year
- **Deadline:** March 15, 2026 (THIS JUST PASSED -- check if late applications accepted or watch for 2027 cycle)
- **URL:** https://www.iamcybersafe.org/s/undergraduate-scholarships
- **Note:** Cash award could be used toward exam fee even if not a direct voucher

### 3. SANS Diversity Cyber Academy (Cyversity + SANS)
- **What:** 100% scholarship: up to 3 SANS courses + GIAC certifications (GSEC, GCIH, etc.) -- not CISSP but extremely high-value certs ($8,000+ value each)
- **Eligibility:** Women and minorities of Black, Latinx, Native American, Asian Pacific backgrounds. College seniors in CS/IT/STEM. Must be US citizen or permanent resident.
- **Process:** Create SANS account, complete 1-hour aptitude assessment (one attempt only), submit application
- **Deadline:** Applications typically open fall (November). Last known deadline November 19, 2024. Watch for 2026 cycle.
- **URL:** https://www.sans.org/cyber-academy/cyversity-sans-diversity-academy/
- **Apply at:** https://application.sans.edu/portal/aca
- **Contact:** immersionacademy@sans.org
- **Your edge:** Black male in cybersecurity, college senior, STEM degree, 4 existing certs = very strong candidate. Even though this is GIAC not CISSP, a GSEC or GCIH adds massive resume value.

### 4. SANS HBCU Cyber Academy
- **What:** Fully funded SANS training + GIAC certification for HBCU students and alumni
- **Application window:** Historically February 1 to March 1
- **URL:** https://www.sans.org/cyber-academy/diversity-academy/
- **Note:** Even if you're not at an HBCU, check eligibility -- some programs extend to broader minority populations

### 5. ISACA Foundation Scholarship (for CISM/CISA path)
- **What:** Financial support for tuition + ISACA Career Building Bundle (1 year ISACA student membership + 1 certification review course)
- **Award:** $1.4M+ awarded to 800+ students since 2022
- **Deadline:** May 5, 2026 (CURRENT -- APPLY NOW)
- **URL:** https://isaca.secure-platform.com/a/page/ISACAfoundation/aboutscholarships
- **Note:** This covers ISACA certs (CISM, CISA, CRISC), not CISSP. But the career building bundle includes cert prep that adds to your portfolio.

## TIER 2: STRONG OPTIONS (Apply within 30 days)

### 6. KnowBe4 Scholarship for Black Americans in Cybersecurity
- **What:** $10,000 scholarship + up to $3,000 for housing = $13,000 total
- **Eligibility:** Black/African-American US citizen, current undergraduate, cybersecurity or info assurance focus, 3.2+ GPA
- **Deadline:** Check for 2026-27 cycle -- 2026 deadline was January 7, 2026 (may have passed)
- **URL:** https://www.knowbe4.com/press/knowbe4-to-offer-10000-black-americans-in-cybersecurity-scholarship-2
- **Status:** May have been discontinued. Verify current availability.

### 7. Cyversity (formerly ICMCP) -- Marci McCarthy Certification Scholarship
- **What:** $5,000 over two years for certification + career advancement. Specifically designed for women and minority cybersecurity professionals to obtain CompTIA certifications.
- **Organization:** Cyversity (rebranded from ICMCP in ~2023)
- **URL:** https://www.cyversity.org/
- **Additional:** Cyversity + Security Innovation partnership offers $1,000 training scholarships to members
- **Action:** Join Cyversity (free membership), then apply for available programs

### 8. ISSA Education Foundation Scholarships
- **What:** Named scholarships from $2,000 to $3,500
  - Howard A. Schmidt Memorial: $3,500 (undergraduate)
  - E. Eugene Schultz Jr. Memorial: $3,500 (graduate)
  - Shon Harris WIS Memorial: $2,000 (women in security)
  - General Scholarship (Docent Institute): $2,000
- **Eligibility:** Full-time student in infosec-related program, sophomore/junior/senior or graduate
- **Materials:** Personal statement, certifications list, resume, extracurricular activities
- **URL:** https://issaef.org/scholarships/apply/
- **Action:** Check current cycle deadlines

### 9. Blacks In Technology (BIT) -- Certification Discounts
- **What:** CompTIA certification discount codes for members, Fortinet training (free, $400 value), SplunkWorks+ training (free)
- **URL:** https://blacksintechnology.org/programs/
- **Action:** Join BIT, complete form for CompTIA discount code. Not CISSP-specific but adds to cert portfolio and saves money on any future CompTIA certs.

### 10. Paller Cybersecurity Scholarship (SANS Technology Institute)
- **What:** Full scholarships for SANS graduate programs including GIAC certifications
- **URL:** https://www.sans.edu/paller-cybersecurity-scholarship/
- **Note:** This is for SANS Technology Institute degree programs, but includes GIAC certs as part of the curriculum

## TIER 3: WORTH EXPLORING

### 11. ISC2 Atlanta Chapter
- **What:** Free membership (just attend a meeting). Networking with local CISSP holders who may know of sponsorship opportunities.
- **Meetings:** Monthly (next one check website)
- **RSVP:** members@atl-isc2.org
- **URL:** https://atl-isc2.org/
- **Action:** Attend next meeting. Ask about local employer sponsorship, study groups, and any chapter-level exam assistance. ISC2 chapters are listed as a legitimate source for exam voucher discounts.

### 12. ISC2 Candidate Program
- **What:** First year free. Get 10% off Official ISC2 Online Self-Paced Training (does NOT discount exam fee). After year 1, $50/year to maintain.
- **URL:** https://www.isc2.org/candidate
- **Savings:** 10% off training courses only. The exam is still $749.
- **Note:** You're already an SSCP holder so you're already an ISC2 member. Candidate program is for newcomers. Check if member discounts apply differently.

### 13. ISC2 Peace of Mind Voucher Bundle
- **What:** $998 for two exam attempts (vs $749 for one). Gives you a free retake within 180 days.
- **Extra cost:** $249 more than single attempt
- **URL:** https://www.isc2.org/landing/exam-peace-of-mind
- **Note:** If you can scrape together $749, this is worth considering if you're not 100% confident. $249 insurance vs $749 for a full retake.

### 14. ISACA Atlanta Chapter
- **What:** Local networking, events, potential sponsorship connections
- **URL:** https://engage.isaca.org/atlantachapter/
- **Action:** Attend events, network for employer sponsorship leads

### 15. CyberCorps Scholarship for Service (SFS)
- **What:** $27,000/year stipend (undergrad) or $37,000/year (grad) + tuition + laptop + books + health insurance
- **Catch:** Must work in federal/state/local government cybersecurity for equal time as scholarship duration
- **Georgia institution:** Augusta University (recruiting Feb 2025 for Fall 2025 start)
- **URL:** https://sfs.opm.gov/
- **Note:** You're graduating May 2026 so this is likely too late for undergrad. But if you pursue a master's, this is a massive funding source. Also, CISA announced 100 SFS cybersecurity internship roles for summer 2026 (applied by Feb 27, 2026 on USAJobs).

### 16. DoD Cyber Scholarship Program (CySP)
- **What:** $27,000/year (undergrad) or $32,000/year (grad) stipend + tuition + books + laptop
- **Catch:** Must work for DoD after graduation
- **Deadline:** Typically February 1 for following academic year
- **URL:** https://dodcio.defense.gov/Portals/0/Documents/Cyber/ScholarshipProgramFastFacts.pdf
- **Note:** Same timing issue -- likely too late for current cycle but viable for grad school

## TIER 5: ALTERNATIVE STRATEGIES

### 20. Employer Sponsorship (Future)
- **What:** Many cybersecurity employers cover CISSP exam fees as part of onboarding or professional development
- **Strategy:** During job interviews, ask about certification reimbursement. Many companies (especially defense contractors, Big 4 consulting, MSSPs) will pay $749 as a signing benefit or within first 90 days.
- **Realistic:** If you land a security role before taking CISSP, negotiate exam reimbursement into your offer letter.

### 21. GoFundMe / Community Crowdfunding
- **What:** Create a campaign explaining your certification goal, existing certs, and financial need
- **Strategy:** Share on LinkedIn, cybersecurity communities, Reddit r/cissp, r/cybersecurity
- **Note:** No specific results found for CISSP crowdfunding success stories, but the cybersecurity community is supportive of people investing in their careers.

### 22. Tax Deduction
- **What:** CISSP exam fee and prep materials may be tax-deductible as professional development expenses
- **URL:** https://claimyr.com/government-services/irs/Tax-Deductions-for-Professional-Certifications-How-to-Claim-Exam-Fees-Prep-Materials/2025-04-11
- **Note:** Doesn't help with upfront cost but recovers money at tax time

### 23. Authorized Training Provider Bundles
- **What:** Some bootcamp providers include exam voucher with course purchase. If someone else (employer, scholarship) pays for training, you get the voucher included.
- **Examples:** Official ISC2 training courses, Infosec Institute, SANS (for GIAC), etc.

### 24. CompTIA Academic Store (for future certs)
- **What:** Discounted exam vouchers for verified students
- **URL:** https://academic-store.comptia.org
- **Note:** Only for CompTIA exams. You already have Security+, but if pursuing CySA+ or CASP+ renewal, student pricing saves money.

### 25. Fortinet NSE Certifications (Free Alternative)
- **What:** All NSE self-paced courses are free. Certifications from NSE 1-8 cover network security concepts.
- **URL:** https://training.fortinet.com/
- **Value:** Free certs to add to resume while pursuing CISSP funding
