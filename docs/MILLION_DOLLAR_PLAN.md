## The 5 Things That Kill This Plan

1. **Not landing a job in months 1-3.** Everything downstream depends on W-2 income and clearance. Mitigation: apply aggressively, expand to GRC/compliance roles (faster hire cycles).

2. **Health collapse.** Sickle cell crisis, ADHD without meds, malnutrition. Mitigation: get back on meds ASAP, eat every day, sleep 7 hours, don't skip gym.

3. **LLC never scales past solo consulting.** If you can't find subcontractors or land contracts, LLC stays at $5K-$10K/month. Mitigation: start networking with cleared professionals NOW.

4. **Federal contracts take too long.** SAM.gov registration, proposals, award cycles can take 12-18 months. Mitigation: target subcontracts first (faster), direct federal contracts second.

5. **Trying to do everything at once and burning out.** Mitigation: the phases exist for a reason. Don't try to do Phase 4 in Phase 1.

The play is: aim for $1M, accept that $250K-$500K is the realistic middle ground, and know that even $100K net worth in 2 years is a massive win.
