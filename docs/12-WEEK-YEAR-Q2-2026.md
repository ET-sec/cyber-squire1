## Emergency Resources (Use These Now)

### Food
- **SNAP/EBT:** Apply at dfcs.georgia.gov (if in GA). Processing takes 7-30 days. Expedited if income is $0.
- **Food banks:** feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank
- **Campus food pantry:** Most universities have one. Check student services.

### Medication (Patient Assistance Programs)
- **Vyvanse (Takeda):** Takeda Help at Hand program. $0 copay if you qualify. takeda.com/patient-assistance
- **Bupropion XL 300mg:** Generic is $15-30/mo on GoodRx or costplusdrugs.com

### Mental Health
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Call or text 988
- **SAMHSA Helpline:** 1-800-662-4357 (free referrals)
- **Campus counseling:** Usually free for enrolled students. Use it.

### Financial Emergency
- **University emergency fund:** Most schools have a Dean of Students emergency fund. Apply.
- **United Way 211:** Dial 211 for local assistance (rent, utilities, food)
