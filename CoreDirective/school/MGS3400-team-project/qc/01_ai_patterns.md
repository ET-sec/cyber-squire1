# Agent 1: AI Pattern Audit

Scope: paper.docx (45 paragraphs reviewed) and slides.pptx (15 slides reviewed). Hunt for AI tell-words, antithesis templates, parallel triplets, generic openers, drama em dashes, vague intensifiers, hedge phrases, and capitalized-noun-phrase labels misused mid-prose.

---

## Findings

### Finding 1
- **Location:** paper paragraph [5]
- **Original:** "The quick-service restaurant (QSR) industry provides a unique lens for examining organizational behavior because front-line employees are the product experience."
- **Issue:** Generic AI opener pattern ("provides a unique lens for examining"). This phrasing is a classic ChatGPT scaffolding move for academic intros.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "In the quick-service restaurant (QSR) industry, front-line employees are the product. Customers do not just buy chicken, they buy the interaction that comes with it."

### Finding 2
- **Location:** paper paragraph [7]
- **Original:** "This structure ensures that every location has a dedicated, hands-on leader who is deeply embedded in the daily operations and employee development of that specific store."
- **Issue:** "deeply embedded" is a vague AI intensifier. Triple adjective stack ("dedicated, hands-on leader who is deeply embedded") reads generated.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Because of this structure, every store has a single owner-operator who works the floor and trains the staff personally."

### Finding 3
- **Location:** paper paragraph [12]
- **Original:** "which is remarkable in an industry where the average exceeds 130% annually"
- **Issue:** "remarkable" is a vague intensifier. AI tic.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "which is striking in an industry where the average exceeds 130% annually" (or just drop the editorializing: "compared to a 130% industry average").

### Finding 4
- **Location:** paper paragraph [12]
- **Original:** "Chick-fil-A employees demonstrated consistent eye contact, proactive helpfulness, and coordinated teamwork during rush periods."
- **Issue:** Triplet parallel list with parallel adjective-noun construction ("consistent eye contact, proactive helpfulness, and coordinated teamwork"). Classic ChatGPT cadence.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Chick-fil-A workers made eye contact, asked customers if they needed help before being asked, and stayed coordinated during the lunch rush."

### Finding 5
- **Location:** paper paragraph [13]
- **Original:** "long wait times, minimal employee-to-customer interaction, and visible signs of workplace stress among staff during peak hours."
- **Issue:** Another triplet parallel list, mirrors the prior paragraph's structure exactly. Two back-to-back triplets is a strong AI signal.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "longer waits, less interaction between staff and customers, and obvious stress on workers during the lunch rush."

### Finding 6
- **Location:** paper paragraph [16]
- **Original:** "The cultural difference between Chick-fil-A and Popeyes is visible at every level."
- **Issue:** "visible at every level" is a stock AI transition phrase that tees up a list.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Chick-fil-A and Popeyes differ at all three levels."

### Finding 7
- **Location:** paper paragraph [17]
- **Original:** "These artifacts signal a culture of discipline, consistency, and service orientation."
- **Issue:** Triplet parallel noun list ("discipline, consistency, and service orientation"). Third triplet in the paper.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "These artifacts signal that the brand values discipline and consistency in how staff treat customers."

### Finding 8
- **Location:** paper paragraph [18]
- **Original:** "the company's commitment to this mission shapes hiring decisions, training programs, and operational priorities."
- **Issue:** Fourth triplet ("hiring decisions, training programs, and operational priorities"). Pattern is now obvious.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "the mission still shapes who Chick-fil-A hires and how it trains them."

### Finding 9
- **Location:** paper paragraph [19]
- **Original:** "the underlying assumption among many front-line workers appears to be that the job is transactional: you show up, complete tasks, and leave."
- **Issue:** Hedge phrase "appears to be" (AI hedging). Also another triplet ("show up, complete tasks, and leave").
- **Suggested rewrite:** "many front-line Popeyes workers seem to treat the job as transactional. Clock in, do the tasks, clock out."

### Finding 10
- **Location:** paper paragraph [22]
- **Original:** "This creates a visible career path that inspires employees to invest in their performance."
- **Issue:** "inspires employees to invest in their performance" is generic corporate-AI phrasing.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Workers can see a real promotion path, which gives them a reason to care about their shifts."

### Finding 11
- **Location:** paper paragraph [22]
- **Original:** "Operators also demonstrate 'idealized influence' by working alongside their teams during rush periods, setting a behavioral example rather than simply issuing directives."
- **Issue:** "setting a behavioral example rather than simply issuing directives" is the antithesis template ("doing X rather than simply Y"). Classic AI rhetoric scaffold.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Operators also model 'idealized influence' by jumping on the line during rush instead of barking orders from the back."

### Finding 12
- **Location:** paper paragraph [23]
- **Original:** "leadership defaults to management by exception, where attention is given primarily when problems arise rather than when things go well."
- **Issue:** Antithesis template again ("when X, rather than when Y").
- **Suggested rewrite:** "leadership defaults to management by exception. Managers only show up when something breaks."

### Finding 13
- **Location:** paper paragraph [26]
- **Original:** "These motivators drive genuine job satisfaction that goes beyond simply not being dissatisfied."
- **Issue:** "goes beyond simply not being X" is an AI antithesis construction.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "These motivators produce real job satisfaction, not just an absence of complaints."

### Finding 14
- **Location:** paper paragraph [27]
- **Original:** "the resulting stress pushes more people out. Without motivators to drive engagement and with hygiene factors causing active dissatisfaction, Popeyes employees are far less likely to deliver the kind of discretionary effort that defines successful customer service."
- **Issue:** Long, balanced compound sentence with paired clauses ("Without X and with Y, Z") is a ChatGPT signature. Also "discretionary effort that defines successful customer service" is vague academic-speak.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "the stress pushes more workers out. With nothing pulling them in and plenty pushing them away, Popeyes staff rarely give the extra effort that good service requires."

### Finding 15
- **Location:** paper paragraph [30]
- **Original:** "This allowed us to observe both companies' employees in comparable market conditions."
- **Issue:** "in comparable market conditions" is corporate-AI filler.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "We could watch staff at both chains under the same local conditions."

### Finding 16
- **Location:** paper paragraph [32]
- **Original:** "they represent three interconnected levels of the integrative model of organizational behavior discussed in our course material."
- **Issue:** "three interconnected levels of the integrative model" is buzzword-dense AI phrasing.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "they cover three connected layers from the organizational behavior model in our textbook."

### Finding 17
- **Location:** paper paragraph [33]
- **Original:** "We considered other elements such as team dynamics and motivation but found that culture, leadership, and satisfaction provided the most explanatory power for the differences we observed."
- **Issue:** "provided the most explanatory power" is academic-AI register, slightly inflated for an undergrad paper.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "We considered team dynamics and motivation too, but culture, leadership, and satisfaction explained the most about what we saw."

### Finding 18
- **Location:** paper paragraph [35]
- **Original:** "The three theories we applied form a coherent analytical chain."
- **Issue:** "form a coherent analytical chain" is exactly how ChatGPT closes a synthesis section. Stock phrase.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "The three theories link up in order."

### Finding 19
- **Location:** paper paragraph [35]
- **Original:** "Together, these theories explain not just what is different between Chick-fil-A and Popeyes, but why those differences exist and how they produce the observable outcomes in employee performance and customer experience."
- **Issue:** Strong "not just X, but Y" antithesis template, plus a triplet expansion ("what is different... why... how"). One of the most obvious AI sentences in the paper. Also closes the body with the AI-favorite "Together, these theories" bridge.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "These three theories show why Chick-fil-A and Popeyes differ and how those differences show up at the counter."

### Finding 20
- **Location:** slide [5] body bullet
- **Original:** "Observable Engagement: In-person visits to Atlanta-area locations revealed stark contrasts in eye contact, teamwork, and service energy"
- **Issue:** "stark contrasts" is AI cliche. Triplet list ("eye contact, teamwork, and service energy"). "Service energy" is a vague AI noun phrase.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "On-site visits in Atlanta: CFA staff made eye contact, worked as a team, and moved with purpose. Popeyes staff did not."

### Finding 21
- **Location:** slide [8] Popeyes assumption bullet
- **Original:** "Show up, do the minimum, leave."
- **Issue:** Three-beat parallel command list, cinematic AI rhythm. (Same pattern shows up in paper P19.)
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Clock in, get through the shift, clock out."

### Finding 22
- **Location:** slide [11] body
- **Original:** "Key Insight: Meeting hygiene factors only prevents dissatisfaction. True satisfaction requires motivators. You need BOTH."
- **Issue:** Three short staccato sentences in a row, escalating to an all-caps drama beat. Classic AI "thought leader" cadence.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Hygiene factors stop people from quitting in disgust. Motivators are what make them care. Both have to be there."

### Finding 23
- **Location:** slide [14] bullet 1
- **Original:** "Employee success is not about the product — it is about how organizations invest in their people"
- **Issue:** Em dash used as a drama beat ("not about X — it is about Y"). Antithesis template. Two violations in one bullet.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Employee success comes from how a company invests in its people, not from the product on the menu."

### Finding 24
- **Location:** slide [14] bullet 2
- **Original:** "Culture must be intentional and reinforced at every level, not just stated in a mission document"
- **Issue:** Antithesis template ("X, not just Y").
- **Suggested rewrite:** "A mission document does not create culture. Daily reinforcement at every level does."

### Finding 25
- **Location:** slide [14] bullet 3
- **Original:** "Transformational leadership produces dramatically different outcomes than transactional management"
- **Issue:** "dramatically different outcomes" is a vague intensifier ("dramatically") plus generic AI noun phrase.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Transformational leaders get different results than transactional ones, and the gap is wide."

### Finding 26
- **Location:** slide [14] bullet 4
- **Original:** "Meeting hygiene factors is table stakes. Real engagement comes from motivators like recognition, growth, and purpose"
- **Issue:** "table stakes" is corporate-AI cliche. Triplet ("recognition, growth, and purpose"). Two-sentence "X. Real Y comes from Z" antithesis.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Pay and clean kitchens are the floor, not the ceiling. Real engagement comes from being recognized and given a path forward."

### Finding 27
- **Location:** slide [14] bullet 5
- **Original:** "The gap between Chick-fil-A and Popeyes is not about chicken. It is about people."
- **Issue:** Pure cinematic antithesis ("It is not X. It is Y."). The single most ChatGPT-coded line in the deck. A professor scanning for AI will stop here.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "Chick-fil-A and Popeyes serve similar food. The gap is in how each company treats the people behind the counter."

### Finding 28
- **Location:** paper paragraphs [16], [21], [25] and slides [7], [9], [11]
- **Original:** "Schein's Three Levels of Culture model", "transformational versus transactional leadership", "Two-Factor Theory", "Idealized Influence", "Inspirational Motivation", "Intellectual Stimulation", "Individualized Consideration", "Contingent Reward", "Management by Exception"
- **Issue:** Repeated capitalized-noun-phrase labels mid-sentence. The first reference is fine because it names the model. Subsequent references in flowing prose should drop the capitals (e.g., "individualized consideration", "contingent reward"). Capitalized labels read like a textbook glossary that an AI pasted in.
- **Suggested rewrite:** Capitalize on first introduction only, then use lowercase: "individualized consideration", "idealized influence", "contingent reward", "management by exception", "two-factor theory", "transformational" and "transactional".

### Finding 29
- **Location:** paper paragraph [12] and slide [5]
- **Original:** "consistently ranks first in the American Customer Satisfaction Index" / "CFA 85/100 (#1)"
- **Issue:** Not an AI tell, but flagging because the phrase "consistently ranks first" is a soft AI intensifier when paired with a single year of data. Either qualify ("ranked first in 2023") or back the word "consistently" with multi-year evidence.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "ranked first in the American Customer Satisfaction Index for limited-service restaurants in 2023, as it has every year since 2014."

### Finding 30
- **Location:** paper paragraph [27]
- **Original:** "High turnover creates a cycle where experienced employees leave, new employees are undertrained, and the resulting stress pushes more people out."
- **Issue:** Classic AI "creates a cycle where X, Y, and Z" formula. Triplet inside a doom-loop framing.
- **Suggested rewrite:** "High turnover feeds itself. Experienced workers leave, new hires get rushed onto the line, and the stress drives more people out the door."

### Finding 31
- **Location:** paper paragraph [22]
- **Original:** "where leaders attend to each follower's needs for achievement and growth."
- **Issue:** "attend to each follower's needs for achievement and growth" is a near-quote of academic AI summary writing. The phrasing is fine in a textbook, but in a student paper it stands out.
- **Suggested rewrite:** Quote Bass directly with a citation, or rewrite plainly: "where leaders pay attention to what each worker needs to grow."

### Finding 32
- **Location:** slide [6] connector line
- **Original:** "Culture sets the environment  →  Leadership transmits it  →  Job Satisfaction is the outcome"
- **Issue:** Three-beat arrow chain with capitalized labels. Reads like an AI-generated framework slide. Same exact structure repeats on slide [13] ("Sets the ENVIRONMENT / TRANSMITS daily / Measurable OUTCOME"). Duplicate framework wording across two slides looks templated.
- **Suggested rewrite:** Vary the wording between the two slides, drop one of them, or use different verbs: "Culture is the soil. Leadership is the gardener. Job satisfaction is what grows."

## SUMMARY

- Total findings: 32
- Highest-risk single line: slide 14 bullet 5 ("It is not about chicken. It is about people."). This one will trigger any professor familiar with AI cadence.
- Most repeated tell: parallel triplet noun lists ("X, Y, and Z") used at least 8 times across the paper and deck.
- Second most repeated tell: antithesis templates ("not X, but Y" / "rather than simply Z") used 7 times.
- Capitalized labels used mid-prose: ~9 instances. Easy fix, large readability win.
- Em dash drama beats: 1 confirmed (slide 14), low overall.
- Generic AI vocabulary from the watch list: "remarkable", "deeply embedded", "stark contrasts", "dramatically", "table stakes", "coherent analytical chain". Six hits.
- None of the explicit watch-list words ("delve", "tapestry", "robust", "leverage", "unlock", "harness", "in the realm of", "moreover", "furthermore", "ultimately", "essentially") appear. Good.

**AI-detection risk score: 6/10**

The factual content, citations, and observation data read human and specific. The risk comes almost entirely from rhythm and rhetorical templates: too many triplets, too many "not X, but Y" reversals, and the cinematic closing line on slide 14. A reader who is not actively hunting will pass this. A reader who is hunting will land on slides 11 and 14 and the closing paragraph of the paper. Fixing the 5 highest-risk items (Findings 19, 22, 23, 26, 27) drops the score to roughly 3/10.
