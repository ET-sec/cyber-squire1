### 15. QSR turnover average (130%+)
- **Location:** paper para [12], paper para [13], slide [5]
- **Claim:** "industry average exceeds 130% annually" attributed to National Restaurant Association, 2023
- **Verification:** The 130-150% range for QSR turnover is widely cited across industry blogs (DailyPay, QSRSoft, Payactiv). However, the National Restaurant Association's own 2023 State of the Restaurant Industry reports a broader restaurant industry turnover of 72.4% in 2023 (which is total restaurants, not QSR specifically). The 130%+ figure for QSRs comes from various industry analytics, not directly from NRA. Citation attribution is questionable.
- **Status:** Inconsistent (number is plausible for QSR specifically, but the source attribution to "National Restaurant Association, 2023" is not directly supported by NRA's published 2023 report)
- **Recommended fix:** Either pull the 130% figure from a QSR-specific source (DailyPay, QSRSoft, or Restaurant Dive) and cite that, or change attribution to a QSR-focused industry source. NRA reports closer to 73% for the broader restaurant industry.
