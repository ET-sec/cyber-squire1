# Agent 3 - Grammar, Spelling, Mechanics, and Clarity QC

Scope: Full proofread of paper.docx and slides.pptx for grammar, mechanics, usage, spelling, capitalization, number formatting, citation consistency, and terminology.

## Findings

### Paper (paper.docx)

---

- **Location:** paper para 5 (intro)
- **Original:** "front-line employees are the product experience"
- **Issue:** Missing article. Reads as a fragment of the noun phrase. Should be "the product experience" with a connecting word, or rephrased so the noun phrase is parallel with the singular subject group.
- **Fix:** "front-line employees are the product experience itself" or "front-line employees deliver the product experience"

---

- **Location:** paper para 5 (intro)
- **Original:** "the speed, friendliness, and accuracy they encounter is a direct reflection"
- **Issue:** Subject-verb agreement. The compound subject "speed, friendliness, and accuracy" is plural and requires "are."
- **Fix:** "the speed, friendliness, and accuracy they encounter are a direct reflection"

---

- **Location:** paper para 7 (Chick-fil-A)
- **Original:** "Chick-fil-A operates over 3,000 locations across the United States"

Acceptable variation, but the paper later uses "over 3,700 locations" for Popeyes (para 9), which is consistent with this style. No action needed beyond noting the slide vs paper style differs slightly ("over" vs "+").

---

- **Location:** paper para 12 (Defining Success)

---

- **Location:** paper para 12 (Defining Success)

---

## Cross-Cutting Findings

1. **Em dash usage in slides** violates project rules of engagement (8 instances across slides 9, 10, 14). Replace with colons or periods.

2. **Person shift (third to second person)** appears 3 times in the paper (paras 19, 23) and once on slide 11. Maintain third person throughout for academic register.

3. **Number formatting inconsistency.** Paper uses "30 to 40%" while slides use "~30-40%." Pick one range style and apply across both deliverables. Same for "$136 million" vs "$136M+" and "3,000" vs "3,000+."

4. **Citation parity gap.** Paper cites ACSI 2023 only once; second use lacks citation. Slide 15 reference list omits publisher and URL information present in paper.

5. **Abbreviation consistency.** Slides use "CFA" without first defining it. If kept, define on first use.

6. **Geographic name style.** Paper spells "Georgia"; slides use "GA." Pick one.

7. **Punctuation style on bullets.** Slide 14 mixes terminal periods. Standardize.

8. **Logic error on slide 5.** Header "Employee Retention" paired with turnover percentages. Change header or convert numbers.
