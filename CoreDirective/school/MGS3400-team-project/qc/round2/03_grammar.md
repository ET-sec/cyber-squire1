3. **Italics on book and report titles (HIGH).** No titles are italicized in either deliverable. APA 7 requires italics for book titles, report titles, and journal article series. Affects all references and any in-text mentions of "Dare to Serve" or "Eat Mor Chikin."

4. **Number formatting consistency (MEDIUM).** "Near 60 percent" (paper) vs "~60%" (slides). Approximation markers vary: "near," "roughly," "~". Pick one for each deliverable type.

5. **Hyphenation: "limited-service" vs "limited service" (MEDIUM).** Paper hyphenates as a compound modifier; slide does not. Pick one.

6. **Operator capitalization (LOW, verified consistent).** Capitalized "Operator" when used as the Chick-fil-A role title (P5, P18, P23, P32; slides 3, 8, 10, 13). Lowercase "operator" when generic ("operator applicants," "operator turnover" in P5 and P9). This convention is internally consistent and grammatically defensible.

7. **Theory term capitalization (LOW, verified consistent).** Title-cased when functioning as a label (slide headers, "Schein's Three Levels of Culture," "Two-Factor Theory"). Lowercase mid-sentence in body text ("transformational leadership," "transactional leadership"). Verified consistent across both files.

8. **Smart quotes (LOW, verified consistent).** Both files use curly quotation marks around "My Pleasure" and around the corporate purpose statement. No straight-quote contamination found.

9. **Em dashes (LOW, verified absent).** Round 1 flagged 8 em dashes across slides 9, 10, and 14. Round 2 confirms zero em dashes in either file.

10. **Sentence length (MEDIUM, mostly clean).** Six sentences exceed 30 words in the paper. Three are acceptable due to embedded data or quoted material. Three (P3 intro, P7 Bachelder, P9 ACSI sentence) are at the upper edge but readable.
