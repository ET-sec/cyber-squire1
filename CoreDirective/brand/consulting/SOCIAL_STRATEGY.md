## 1. Brand Overview

### Identity

CoreDirective is a cybersecurity education and automation brand built by Emmanuel Tigoue -- a security engineer with active SecurityX (CASP+), SSCP, CCNA, Security+, and Network+ certifications (CISSP in progress). The brand operates at the intersection of three pillars:

| Pillar | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| Security Engineering | The core expertise. Vulnerability analysis, compliance, zero trust architecture. |
| AI Automation | n8n SOAR pipelines, AI agents (OpenClaw, Ollama), Terraform IaC, cloud security tooling. |
| Tactical Content | Teaching cybersecurity through live streams, short-form video, study systems, and community. |

**Tagline:** Signal over noise.
**Sub-tagline:** Security Engineering, AI Automation, and Tactical Content.
**Brand line:** Building systems. Breaking them down. Teaching both.

### Visual Identity

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Primary color | Electric blue `#3B82F6` on black void background |
| Typography | JetBrains Mono (headings, code, technical) + Inter (body, UI) |
| Design philosophy | Tactical restraint. Every element earns its place. No decoration for its own sake. |
| Tone signal | Competence through clarity, not volume. The brand looks like it was built by an engineer, not a marketing team. |

### Voice and Tone

- **Direct and tactical.** No fluff, no filler, no motivational slop.
- **Teaches instead of sells.** Content leads. Products exist because they solve a specific problem.
- **No hype vocabulary.** Never "crushing it," never "10x," never "unlock your potential." Say what it is. Say what it does.
- **Accessible over exclusive.** Pricing reflects this. Language reflects this. The free tier is genuinely useful.
- **Operative phrase:** "The information exists. We just organized it."

## 2. Content Strategy

### Production Schedule

| Day | Time (ET) | Format | Title / Description |
|-----|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Mon | Daily | TikTok Short | Tactical Breakdown (60-90 sec) -- single concept, single takeaway |
| Tue | Daily + 8:00 PM | TikTok Short + LIVE | Tactical Breakdown + **Threat Brief LIVE** -- vulnerability/exploit/compliance deep-dive |
| Wed | Daily | TikTok Short | Tactical Breakdown |
| Thu | Daily + 8:00 PM | TikTok Short + LIVE | Tactical Breakdown + **The Build LIVE** -- live infrastructure builds, automation, security engineering |
| Fri | Daily + 8:00 PM | TikTok Short + LIVE | Tactical Breakdown + **Open Q&A** -- career, certs, technical questions, industry discussion |
| Sat | Daily (+ optional LIVE) | TikTok Short | Tactical Breakdown (Q&A overflow if needed) |
| Weekly | 3x | YouTube Long-Form | Briefings -- 8-15 min produced videos on security topics, tool walkthroughs, cert prep |

**Live streams multistream** to TikTok + YouTube simultaneously via OBS.

### Repurpose Chain

Every piece of live content feeds downstream formats. Nothing is produced once and abandoned.

```
LIVE STREAM (Tue/Thu/Fri)
    |
    +---> Clip to TikTok Verticals (next-day shorts)
    |
    +---> Transcribe (Faster-Whisper)
    |         |
    |         +---> LinkedIn Article (edited, professional tone)
    |         |
    |         +---> Newsletter Content (weekly digest)
    |         |
    |         +---> Skool Discussion Prompts (community engagement)
    |
    +---> YouTube Long-Form Edit (combine best segments into briefing)
```

**Weekly output targets:**
- 6 TikTok shorts (daily tactical breakdowns)
- 3 live streams (Tue/Thu/Fri)
- 3 YouTube long-form briefings
- 1-2 LinkedIn articles (derived from transcripts)
- 2-3 Skool discussion threads (derived from Q&A and builds)

## 3. Platform Strategy

Each platform has a specific role. No cross-posting identical content -- every platform gets native formatting.

| Platform | Handle | Role | Content Type | Posting Cadence |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **TikTok** | @coredirective.official | Discovery + top-of-funnel | 60-90 sec tactical breakdowns, live streams | Daily shorts + 3x live/week |
| **YouTube** | @coredirective.official | Depth + long-term value | 8-15 min briefings, full live stream archives | 3x long-form/week + live |
| **LinkedIn** | emmanuel-tigoue | Professional credibility + recruiter visibility | Articles, career insights, cert breakdowns, project showcases | 2-3x/week |
| **GitHub** | ET-sec | Technical proof + portfolio | Open-source projects, IaC configs, portfolio site | As shipped |
| **Skool** | The InnerDirective | Community + retention + revenue | Study groups, Q&A threads, cert prep, career guidance | Daily engagement |
| **Gumroad** | coredirective1 | Product delivery | Notion study templates | Evergreen (updated per cert cycle) |

### Platform-Specific Notes

- **TikTok** is the volume play. Algorithmic discovery. Hook in 3 seconds. One concept per video. CTA to bio link (Linktree).
- **YouTube** is the trust play. People searching "Security+ study plan" or "n8n SOAR tutorial" find long-form content that demonstrates real competence. SEO matters here.
- **LinkedIn** is the credibility play. Recruiter-facing. Every post reinforces "security engineer who builds and teaches." No motivational content. Technical articles and project breakdowns only.
- **Skool** is the retention play. Free tier builds community. Pro tier generates recurring revenue. Content here is exclusive -- not repurposed social posts, but structured study support and direct access.

## 5. Community Strategy

**Platform:** Skool (skool.com/the-innerdirective)
**Philosophy:** Not a Discord with 1,000 lurkers. A focused community of people actually building cybersecurity careers.

### Tier Structure

| Tier | Price | Access |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Free** | $0 | Community feed, study groups, Q&A threads, career guidance, weekly discussion prompts |
| **Pro** | $47.99/mo | Everything in Free + all Notion study templates (Net+, Sec+, CCNA) + future templates as released |
| **Cyber Squire** (future) | $79.99-89.99/mo | Everything in Pro + cyber range access + structured labs + direct support |

### Community Operating Model

- **Daily engagement:** Respond to posts, seed discussion threads from live stream topics.
- **Weekly study groups:** Aligned with cert exam objectives. Members post progress, ask questions, share resources.
- **Live stream integration:** Q&A streams (Fri) pull questions directly from Skool threads. Members see their questions answered live.
- **Career support:** Resume reviews, interview prep discussion, job market intel. Practical, not theoretical.
- **No gatekeeping:** Free tier is genuinely useful. Pro adds convenience (all templates in one place) and ongoing updates.

## 6. Target Audience

### Primary Segments

| Segment | Description | Entry Point | Likely Conversion |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Career changers** | Professionals pivoting into cybersecurity from IT, military, unrelated fields | TikTok/YouTube (discovery) -> Free Net+ download | Skool Free -> Pro -> Cert products |
| **Early-career security pros** | 0-3 years in security, actively studying for certs | YouTube (cert-specific search) -> Gumroad products | Direct product purchase -> Skool Pro |
| **IT professionals upskilling** | Sysadmins, network engineers, helpdesk adding security to their stack | LinkedIn articles -> YouTube long-form | Gumroad products or Skool Pro |
| **Small business owners** | Need automation, security consulting, or both | LinkedIn/referral -> consulting inquiry | AI & Auto Ops consulting engagement |

### Audience Profile

- **Age range:** 22-40
- **Geography:** US primary, global secondary (English-speaking)
- **Mindset:** Self-directed learners. Skeptical of hype. Want structured, practical resources -- not motivation.
- **Budget sensitivity:** High. Most are studying while working or transitioning. Free and affordable options matter.
- **Decision trigger:** Demonstrated competence. They follow people who clearly know the material, not people who just talk about it.

## Summary

CoreDirective runs on one principle: demonstrate competence, then offer tools that match. Every piece of content proves the expertise. Every product solves a specific problem. Every tier is priced so the person who needs it most can still access it.

The funnel is simple: free content builds trust, a free product captures contact info, a free community builds relationships, and paid tiers exist for people who want structure and convenience. Consulting sits at the top for businesses that want implementation, not education.

No hype. No filler. Signal over noise.
