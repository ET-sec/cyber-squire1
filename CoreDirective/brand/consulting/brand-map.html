06

Platform Map

Top of Funnel

TikTok

Growth engine. 60 second tactical breakdowns that hook new viewers and drive them to longer content. Highest reach, lowest barrier.

SEO and Depth

YouTube

Evergreen library. Long form builds, threat briefs, and Shorts. Search discovery brings people in months after publishing.

Professional Network

LinkedIn

Client and recruiter facing. Professional tone videos on career, certs, and industry trends. Drives AI/Auto Ops consulting leads.

Retention and Community

Skool

Where the audience becomes a community. Daily discussions, challenges, and direct access. Free tier builds trust, paid tiers deliver premium value.

Product Sales

Gumroad

Digital product storefront. Study systems with email capture on the free Net+ download. Entry point to the funnel for certification students.

Portfolio and Credibility

GitHub

Public proof of work. Terraform configs, Docker stacks, workflow automations. Shows recruiters and clients the engineering is real.

Brand Hub

Website (tigouetheory.com)

Central hub that ties everything together. Hero, pillars, live schedule, product links, and community CTA. The one link that represents the entire operation.
