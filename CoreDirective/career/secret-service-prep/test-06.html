SECTION C: Logic-Based Reasoning (21 through 30)

21. A flight DEPARTS ON TIME IF AND ONLY IF the flight crew is ready AND the runway is clear. Flight 227 DID depart on time this morning. Which conclusion MUST follow?

  • A. The crew was ready, but runway status cannot be determined.
  • B. The runway was clear, but crew status cannot be determined.
  • C. The crew was ready AND the runway was clear.
  • D. At least one of the two conditions was satisfied.
  • E. Departure happened as an exception; neither condition was required.

22. Six items (A, B, C, D, E, F) are placed in six positions numbered 1 through 6. Constraints: (1) B is in position 2; (2) D is in position 5; (3) E and F must occupy adjacent positions; (4) C must come somewhere before D; (5) A is NOT in position 1. Which order is consistent with ALL constraints?

  • A. C, B, E, F, D, A
  • B. A, B, E, F, D, C
  • C. C, B, F, D, E, A
  • D. B, C, E, F, D, A
  • E. C, B, F, E, A, D

23. Forty-five incoming calls are distributed among exactly seven dispatch operators. Every call is handled by exactly one operator. What is the MINIMUM number of calls that the busiest operator must handle?

  • A. 6
  • B. 7
  • C. 8
  • D. 9
  • E. Cannot determine without more information

24. Three suspects are interviewed: Adams, Brooks, and Carr. EXACTLY TWO are telling the truth. Their statements:
• Adams: "Brooks is lying."
• Brooks: "Carr is lying."
• Carr: "Adams is telling the truth."
Which pair is telling the truth?

  • A. Adams and Brooks
  • B. Adams and Carr
  • C. Brooks and Carr
  • D. Each of the three possibilities is consistent
  • E. No pair satisfies the constraint

25. "If Gate A is open OR Gate B is open, then the perimeter alarm sounds." On the night of incident #88, the perimeter alarm did NOT sound. Which conclusion MUST follow?

  • A. Gate A alone is closed.
  • B. Gate B alone is closed.
  • C. Both Gate A and Gate B are closed.
  • D. At least one of the gates is closed.
  • E. Nothing specific about the gates can be concluded.

26. "At exactly ONE of two outposts (North OR South) is a red alert active at any given time; never both simultaneously, never neither." Central confirms that the North outpost IS currently on red alert. Which conclusion MUST follow?

  • A. South is also on red alert.
  • B. South is NOT on red alert.
  • C. South's status cannot be determined without more information.
  • D. Exactly one outpost is always on red alert, but which is ambiguous.
  • E. The constraint produces a contradiction.
