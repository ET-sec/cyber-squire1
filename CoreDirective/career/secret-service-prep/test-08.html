SECTION C: Logic-Based Reasoning (21 through 30)

21. Department regulation: "Holding a valid TS/SCI clearance is a NECESSARY condition for working in the Secure Operations Center (SOC)." Officer Park works in the SOC. Which conclusion MUST follow?

  • A. Officer Park holds a valid TS/SCI clearance.
  • B. Every agent with TS/SCI clearance works in the SOC.
  • C. Officer Park is the only person in the SOC.
  • D. TS/SCI clearance is sufficient to work in the SOC.
  • E. Nothing about Officer Park's clearance can be concluded.

22. Five officers (P, Q, R, S, T) are assigned to exactly three fixed posts: NORTH, EAST, SOUTH. Each post must have AT LEAST ONE officer. Constraints: (1) P and Q cannot be at the same post; (2) R and S MUST be at the same post; (3) T is assigned to NORTH alone (no one else is at NORTH). Which assignment is consistent with ALL constraints?

  • A. NORTH: T. EAST: P, R, S. SOUTH: Q.
  • B. NORTH: T, P. EAST: R, S. SOUTH: Q.
  • C. NORTH: T. EAST: R. SOUTH: P, Q, S.
  • D. NORTH: P. EAST: T, R, S. SOUTH: Q.
  • E. NORTH: T. EAST: P, Q. SOUTH: R, S.
