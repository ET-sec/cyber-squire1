SECTION C: Logic-Based Reasoning (21 through 30)

21. Department policy states: "ONLY incidents occurring in Zone A or Zone B alert Dispatch." On Monday night, Dispatch WAS alerted. Which conclusion MUST follow?

  • A. The incident occurred specifically in Zone A.
  • B. The incident occurred specifically in Zone B.
  • C. The incident occurred in Zone A or Zone B (or both).
  • D. The incident occurred OUTSIDE Zones A and B.
  • E. Nothing about the incident location can be concluded.

22. Seven items (P, Q, R, S, T, U, V) are placed in positions 1 through 7. Constraints: (1) P is in position 3; (2) Q is immediately after S; (3) T is before P; (4) U is in position 7; (5) R is NOT in position 1; (6) V is in position 6. Which order satisfies ALL constraints?

  • A. T, R, P, S, Q, V, U
  • B. P, T, R, S, Q, V, U
  • C. T, R, S, Q, P, V, U
  • D. R, T, P, S, Q, V, U
  • E. T, R, P, Q, S, V, U

23. Thirty-seven investigations are assigned to exactly five special agents. Every investigation is assigned to exactly one agent. What is the MINIMUM number of investigations the agent with the highest caseload must handle?

  • A. 6
  • B. 7
  • C. 8
  • D. 9
  • E. 10

24. Three witnesses are interviewed: Franklin, Garcia, and Hayes. EXACTLY ONE is telling the truth (the other two are lying). Their statements:
• Franklin: "Garcia is lying."
• Garcia: "Hayes is lying."
• Hayes: "Both Franklin and Garcia are lying."
Which witness is telling the truth?

  • A. Franklin
  • B. Garcia
  • C. Hayes
  • D. Each possibility is consistent
  • E. No assignment satisfies the constraint
