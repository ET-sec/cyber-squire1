## Never-say list

- "I'm a quick learner" (everyone says this, it lands as filler)
- "I'm passionate about AI" (vague, sets off recruiter alarm)
- "I'm transitioning into" (claims the destination is not yours yet)
- "I'm willing to learn" (lower than "I run this in production")
- "I'm flexible on rate" (kills your floor)
- "I dabble in" (downgrade language)
- "Honestly" repeated (tic, undermines the one time you mean it)
- "I think" (replace with "I do" or "I believe")
- "Just" as a downgrade word ("I just did X")
- "Founder" / "creator" of CoreDirective (Emmanuel is **employee** at CoreDirective, not founder)
- "W2 ready" or any employment-type framing in replies (per locked rule)
- "$5 / $10 budget" anchoring (per locked rule)
- "AI replaces analysts" framing (per locked rule)

## Atmosphere read

If Savannah is:
- **Quick + agenda-driven:** Match her pace. Give 30s pitches. Skip the 2-min unless invited.
- **Conversational + curious:** Open the AI sec body of work. Volunteer the OpenClaw + n8n architecture.
- **Hesitant on technical depth:** Stay high-level. Use mirror language only.
- **Pushing on rate down:** Hold the floor. "Tell me more about the client" and reverse the question.

If she names the end client unprompted: pause, take notes, then prep before the next round.
If she refuses to name the end client even after probing: that is data. Note it in the tracker as a yellow flag.
