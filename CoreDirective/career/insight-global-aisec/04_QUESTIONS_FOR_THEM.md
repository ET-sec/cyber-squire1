# 04 — Questions for Savannah

## Tier 1: ASK THESE (pick 3-4 for index card)

1. **Who is the end client?** "Even if you can't share the name pre-submission, can you tell me the industry, headcount, and where the security team sits in the org?"

2. **What's the pay range and bill rate?** "I'm targeting $95 an hour W2. Where does the client's bill rate sit, and what's your margin range so we're working from the same number?"

3. **Is this remote-permanent or remote-with-relocation-later?** "The JD says remote. Some IG remote contracts shift to hybrid after 90 days. Where does this one sit?"

4. **Why is this a 1-year contract instead of a perm hire?** "That tells me a lot about the program — is this a stand-up effort racing the August 2026 EU AI Act deadline, or a backfill for someone who left, or budget timing?"

5. **What does the interview process look like after this screen?** "How many rounds, who's on each, what's the typical timeline from submit to offer?"

## Tier 2: NICE TO HAVE (ask if time)

6. **Who are the hiring managers and what's their background?** "Specifically — does the HM come from the security side, the AI side, or governance / risk side? That changes how I'd prep."

7. **What's the team's current AI security maturity?** "Are they greenfield-building this practice, or are they hardening an existing program?"

8. **Why this role, why now?** "What was the trigger — incident, audit finding, regulator pressure, AI rollout?"

9. **What does success look like at 90 days, 6 months, 12 months?** "If I hit the bar perfectly, what does that look like in concrete deliverables?"

10. **What's the team size and structure?** "How big is the security org? Does the AI sec function sit inside SecOps, AppSec, GRC, or as its own unit?"

## Tier 3: DEEPER SIGNAL (ask if she invites a longer conversation)

11. **Have you placed at this client before?** "What was the experience? Any patterns I should know about?"

12. **What gets you excited about Insight Global's pipeline right now?** "What kind of AI security work are you seeing land most often?"

## Trap questions to AVOID asking

- "What's your commission structure?" (puts her on guard)
- "Is the client real?" (insulting)
- "Why hasn't this role been filled yet?" (sounds defensive)
- "Will I get benefits?" (ask after the rate is settled)
- "Do you know if the client likes contractors over perm?" (she will not know)
