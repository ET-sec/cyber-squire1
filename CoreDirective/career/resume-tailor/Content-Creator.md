# Content Creator Strategy - Session State

## LINKEDIN CONTENT STRATEGY (Research-Backed, 2026 Algorithm)

### Posting Schedule
- **Frequency:** 3-4 posts/week (optimal per 2026 data)
- **Best days:** Tuesday through Thursday
- **Best time:** 7-9 AM in audience timezone
- **Avoid:** 12-2 PM (oversaturated feeds)
- **Critical window:** First 60 minutes determines amplification

### Content Formats (Ranked by 2026 Performance)
| Format | Engagement Rate | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| PDF Carousels | 6.60% | BEST. 596% more engagement than text-only. 2-3x dwell time |
| Short Video (<90 sec) | ~5.5% | Hook in first 4 seconds. More effort |
| Text-Only | ~4.0% | Strong reach if well-formatted with hooks |
| Single Image + Text | ~4.85% | BUT 30% less REACH than text-only (counterintuitive) |
| External Links | -60% reach | NEVER put links in post body - use first comment |
| Polls | High comments, low reach | Skip |

### Content Ideas for Security Engineers
1. **Incident Debrief carousels** - sanitized IR walkthroughs
2. **Tool comparison posts** - "CrowdStrike vs SentinelOne" type analysis
3. **MITRE ATT&CK breakdowns** - map attack technique to detection rules
4. **"What I got wrong" posts** - failure stories outperform success stories
5. **Certification journey posts** - "I just passed [cert]. Here's what helped."
6. **Security hot takes** - commentary on major breaches, CVEs, policy changes
7. **Career advice for juniors** - "How I went from helpdesk to security engineer"
8. **Newsletter** - one consultant gained 4,800 subscribers in 3 months with "Security Bytes" newsletter, 12K avg reach vs 800 regular posts

### Hashtag Strategy
- Use 1-3 per post (more than 5 triggers spam filter)
- Best: #Cybersecurity, #InfoSec, #CloudSecurity, #IncidentResponse, #ThreatIntelligence
- Niche hashtags > broad hashtags for engagement

### Algorithm Rules (2026)
- Comments are 8x more valuable than likes
- 50 comments > 500 likes for reach
- Thoughtful multi-sentence comments > one-word ("Great!")
- Dwell time is a primary quality signal (why carousels win)
- Personal profiles get 65% of feed consumption vs 5% for company pages
- Niche expertise is rewarded - "AWS IAM misconfigurations" reaches cloud sec pros specifically
- Content lifespan: 48 hours (up from hours)
- LinkedIn deprioritized video in 2026

### Engagement Strategy
- Comment on 3-5 security posts daily (higher ROI than creating content when starting)
- Target: hiring managers and CISOs at target companies
- Your comments appear in THEIR connections' feeds
- Quality commenting > posting frequency when below 100 followers
