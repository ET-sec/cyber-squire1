## Gotcha responses

**If Amit pushes hard on rate cut:** "I understand you have a margin to manage. The rate is locked. If MS sees a value mismatch at $72, I would rather talk to the HM about the value than negotiate the rate down. The work I do is worth $72."

**If Amit pushes for an immediate commitment to start date:** "Start date is a conversation after the HM round. Today I am here to learn the timeline and the conversion path."

**If Amit hints the HM wants someone cheaper:** "If MS has a different rate envelope, that should come from MS. I am the worker, you are the agency, MS is the buyer. Get me in front of the HM and let them assess fit. If they want to negotiate from there, we can talk."

**If Amit mentions other candidates:** "I am sure you have a strong pool. I am confident in my work and ready to interview."

**If he goes silent or vague on conversion:** "Conversion is the key reason I'm taking a C2H. Help me understand what range to plan for, even directionally. If the band is undefined, that's information I need."
