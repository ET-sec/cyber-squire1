# 06 - Questions For Them

Tiered: tier 1 you ASK today, tier 2 nice-to-have today or hold for Zoom, tier 3 deeper signal for HM round.

---

## Tier 1: ASK AMIT SHARMA today (must-have)

1. **Hybrid schedule:** "What is the on-site cadence at Edison Drive? Days per week, any anchor days, any travel beyond Alpharetta?"

2. **MS Hiring Manager:** "Who is the Morgan Stanley HM I will meet on the Zoom? Title? Team name within Cybersecurity?"

3. **Background check vendor and timeline:** "Who runs the background check? First Advantage, HireRight, Checkr? FINRA fingerprinting required? What is the typical turnaround?"

4. **Conversion FTE band:** "What is the typical conversion salary range for senior AI Security or AppSec engineers at MS Alpharetta when contracts convert to FTE? Even a directional band helps me plan."

5. **RTR exclusivity scope:** "The RTR I signed on May 14 was for this specific MS AI Security role. Does it block me from other AI Security or AppSec reqs at MS that other firms might present, or is it strictly per-req?"

6. **Russell Tobin W2 benefits package:** "What are the actual W2 contractor benefits during the contract phase? Holidays paid? Health insurance options? PTO accrual?"

7. **Interview timeline:** "When will the Zoom screen be scheduled? Same week? Next week? What is the expected gap to the on-site? Total time from today to a hire decision?"

---

## Tier 2: ask if time permits, otherwise hold for Zoom

8. **OT / weekend / holiday rate:** "Is there an overtime multiplier or flat rate for weekends, on-call, holidays?"

9. **Equipment provisioning:** "Does MS provide a laptop or do I bring my own? When does provisioning happen relative to start date?"

10. **Conversion timing trigger:** "What event triggers conversion to FTE? A fixed contract end date? Manager request? Performance review milestone?"

11. **MS team size:** "How big is the AI Security or AppSec team at Alpharetta?"

12. **Other Russell Tobin candidates in this req:** "Am I the only RT candidate for this specific role, or am I one of several being submitted?"

---

## Tier 3: hold for MS HM Zoom (deeper signal)

13. **HM background:** "What is your background and what brought you to MS?"

14. **Team composition:** "How is the team structured? Senior IC heavy? Mix of contractor and FTE? Who do you partner with most often?"

15. **First 90 days:** "If I am the right hire, what does success look like in the first 90 days?"

16. **Biggest open problem:** "What is the most pressing AI security problem the team is working on right now that you would want me on?"

17. **Decision authority:** "How much autonomy does an IC have here on which findings to escalate, which to defer, which controls to implement?"

18. **AI program integration:** "How does the AI Security team interact with the AI@MS Assistant and Debrief teams? Are you embedded with them or a parallel function?"

19. **Tooling roadmap:** "What is the current state of your AppSec tooling and where are you trying to take it in the next 12-18 months?"

20. **Compliance posture:** "How does the team work with FINRA, SOX, and GLBA compliance teams? Do AppSec findings tie directly to regulatory artifacts?"

21. **Engineering culture:** "How do engineering teams typically respond when AppSec brings a critical finding? Friendly partner or adversarial?"

22. **Career path:** "What does growth look like for someone in this role? Track to senior, principal, manager?"

23. **Team rituals:** "What are the team rituals: stand-ups, retros, on-call rotation, postmortem culture?"

24. **Why this role is open:** "Is this a backfill or a new headcount? If backfill, what happened with the prior person?"

25. **What would make the perfect candidate:** "If you could write the description of the perfect candidate for this role, what would the top three traits be?"

---

## Tier 4: deeper signal once offer is on the table

26. RSU vesting schedule and refresh cadence
27. Performance bonus formula and targets
28. Parental leave policy and reality
29. Internal mobility policy after FTE conversion
30. Training and conference budget
