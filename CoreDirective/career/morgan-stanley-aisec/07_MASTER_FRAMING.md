## Never-say list

- "I am W2 ready" (employment type is compliance, not pitch)
- "I am immediately available" (anchors desperation)
- "I am flexible on rate" (rate is locked, do not signal flex)
- "I am transitioning into AI security" (you are doing it now)
- "I am pivoting from network engineering" (you are not pivoting)
- "I am bridging into this role" (no bridging language)
- "I want to leverage my background" (kill the word leverage)
- "I will navigate the complex landscape" (kill navigate, kill landscape)
- "I am passionate about cybersecurity" (boilerplate, never say)
- "I'm a problem solver" (boilerplate, never say)
- "I am between roles right now" (CoreDirective is the role)
- Em dashes, en dashes, hyphenated sentence separators (writing rule)
- "Not X. Y." juxtaposition phrasing
- Words to kill: leveraging, delve, robust, comprehensive, seamless, synergy, journey, navigate, unlock, empower, elevate
