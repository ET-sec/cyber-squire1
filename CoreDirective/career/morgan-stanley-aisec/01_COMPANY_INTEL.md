# 01 - Company Intel: Morgan Stanley (Alpharetta hub)

**Sourcing rule:** Every claim below cites a public source at the bottom. Verified 2026-06-08.

---

## 1. Company overview
Morgan Stanley is a global financial services firm with three core franchises: Institutional Securities (investment banking + sales and trading), Wealth Management, and Investment Management. Public company on NYSE under ticker MS. Headquartered at 1585 Broadway, New York City. The firm has approximately 80,000 employees globally and is a FINRA-registered broker-dealer plus a bank holding company.

## 2. Alpharetta hub (your worksite)
Morgan Stanley occupies 216,000 sq ft across The Edison Alpharetta complex at 1 and 3 Edison Drive, under a long-term lease that was expanded by 100,000 sq ft in late 2024. About 2,500 in-town employees, more than 3,000 including the nearby Halcyon site. This makes MS the largest resident employer in Alpharetta. E*TRADE Financial Corporate Services is co-located at 3 Edison Drive. The local team covers software development and engineering, data science and analytics, cloud computing and infrastructure, cybersecurity, product and project management, and AI and robotics.

Implication for you: this is a working tech hub, not a sales branch. The role you are interviewing for is real engineering, not advisor support.

## 3. Security organization
Global Chief Information Security Officer: Alonzo Ellis, Managing Director. He came in from Vanguard where he was CISO; 25+ years across major financial institutions. The Fusion Resilience Center is the firm's 24x7 global threat operations capability. Cyber risk reports up to a dedicated board committee on Operations and Technology.

For AppSec specifically, public job postings show the firm uses Wiz (cloud security), Checkmarx (SAST), Snyk (SCA/SAST/container), with vulnerability prioritization based on CVSS plus EPSS. Primary application stack is Java with Python tooling. Open postings mention building automated triage and remediation workflows using Python and AI-assisted agents.

## 4. AI program (this is the room you're walking into)
Morgan Stanley partnered with OpenAI in March 2023 and rolled out the AI@Morgan Stanley Assistant in September 2023. Wealth Management financial advisors adopted at 98%. Follow-on tools: AI@MS Debrief (meeting notes plus action items), AskResearchGPT (first GenAI application in Institutional Securities). In June 2026, CNBC reported MS is opening its wealth management funnel to autonomous AI agents from corporate clients, allowing third-party agents to pull data directly from MS stock administration platforms.

CEO Ted Pick addressed AI cybersecurity on the Q1 2026 earnings call, noting the firm is working with Mythos Preview on cybersecurity around AI frontier models. The firm has also publicly warned about a major AI breakthrough by 2026.

Implication: the work in your JD (research AI security risks, build AI defenses, analyze attack patterns) sits inside a real, deployed AI program that already serves advisors and is opening to autonomous agents. This is not theoretical work.

## 5. Cloud and infrastructure
Strategic partnership with Microsoft brings together Azure and Morgan Stanley technology teams. Microsoft provides MS a secure and compliant platform for the firm's cloud transformation. Reasonable inference: significant Azure-resident workloads. Splunk and CrowdStrike are common at peer banks; not yet publicly confirmed for MS but worth asking about.

## 6. Regulatory posture
MS is a FINRA-registered broker-dealer plus a bank holding company. That means SEC Rule 17f-2 fingerprinting for staff with access to securities or monies. Sole FINRA fingerprint vendor is First Advantage / Sterling Identity. SOX, GLBA, FFIEC, and state-level financial services regulations all apply. Cybersecurity is overseen by a dedicated board committee.

Implication for the conversation: regulatory fluency wins. If you mention controls aligned with SOX or GLBA when describing your GRC work at CoreDirective, that lands.

## 7. Recent news (last 90 days)
- June 2026: CNBC reports MS will open Wealth Management funnel to autonomous AI agents
- Q1 2026 earnings call: CEO discussed AI cybersecurity, mentioned Mythos Preview collaboration
- 2026 MS Research: AI market trends report
- 2025: MS advised Meta on $27B AI data-center JV; originated 14 private AI company investments via WM Private Markets

No public layoffs, breach disclosures, or regulatory actions in the cybersecurity org surfaced in research.

## 8. Stability and risk
Top-tier IB and WM franchise. Long-term Alpharetta lease. Public dividend payer. No near-term layoff signal in MS Cybersecurity org. Standard financial services downside risks (interest rate, market volatility, regulatory) but no acute idiosyncratic issues.

## 9. Cultural read
Regulated. Compliance-heavy. Long tenure prized. Conservative dress code on-site (business casual minimum). Glassdoor interview feedback for the Alpharetta site in January 2026 (AI-related role) noted friendly, professional interviewers who sent an interview summary email beforehand. Cyber Security Analyst path: 3 rounds (2 technical + 1 HR), 50% positive, 3/5 difficulty. Overall MS interview difficulty 3/5, average 27 days from first contact to offer.
