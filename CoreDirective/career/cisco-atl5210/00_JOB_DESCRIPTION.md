## Job Description (verbatim)

Design and maintain a secure and high-availability privileged access management solution. Implement and manage CyberArk components to streamline and secure access to critical systems. Implement robust security measures to safeguard sensitive credentials and privileged accounts. Oversee both on-premises and cloud-based CyberArk implementations, including components such as Vault, DR Vault, Central Policy Manager (CPM), Privileged Session Manager (PSM), Privileged Session Manager Proxy (PSMP), and Privileged Threat Analytics (PTA). Design and implement secret management tool and platform such as CyberArk Conjur, AWS secret manager, Hashicorp vault. Ensure the Privileged Access Management (PAM) solution can adapt to changing business needs and regulatory requirements. Oversee account management and Group Policy configurations. Handle AD Schema, PKI, and automate PIM processes using PowerShell and Python. Understand and manage protocols such as TCP, UDP, DNS, NetBIOS, HTTP, HTTPS, SMTP, SNMP, SSH, and TLS. Design, implement, and troubleshoot complex network systems including routing, switching, and firewall technologies. Manage CyberArk Credential Provider (CP) and Central Credential Provider (CCP) to support application needs. Manage Windows and Linux Systems which hosts critical Privileged access applications and other environments, Active Directory, IIS, and LDAPS. Assist in planning, estimation and scheduling of CyberArk-related projects and tasks. Work with authentication standards such as SAML, OAuth, and SCIM. Integrate these standards with Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) and access

## Strategic Read — PERM Filing Indicators (HIGH CONFIDENCE)
1. **"Five (5) years of post-baccalaureate, progressive experience in job offered"** — verbatim Department of Labor PERM language. Real Cisco postings use "X+ years" not "(5) years of post-baccalaureate, progressive."
2. **"Bachelor's or foreign degree equivalent"** — only appears on PERM filings (covering foreign-credentialed candidate already in the role).
3. **65-day silence then revival** — fits PERM advertising window. Cisco must document recruitment effort (post job, accept applications, demonstrate no qualified US candidates) before filing.
4. **Hyper-specific stack (CyberArk PSM/PSMP/PTA/CPM/Conjur/CP/CCP)** — narrow enough to match one incumbent's resume exactly. Generic Cisco InfoSec roles don't enumerate this much CyberArk depth.
5. **AMS shared mailbox, not a named recruiter** — PERM workflow routes through AMS (Application Management Services) compliance team, not standard talent acquisition.

**Conclusion:** ~85% likely this is a PERM labor certification posting. The "incumbent" is already in seat; recruiter screen is compliance theater.
