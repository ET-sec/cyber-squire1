## Atlanta Cloudflare AppSec market (2026)

- **Senior AppSec base:** ~$178k median ($86/hr equiv), 75th percentile $214k (Glassdoor / Salary.com)
- **W2 contract rate (senior, with Cloudflare WAF chops):** $90-115/hr, $105-135/hr 1099/C2C
- **Anchor:** $105/hr W2, walk below $85
- **Markup math:** typical IT staffing markup 35-50% on W2. Client bill rate $150/hr typically clears ~$95-100 W2
- **Conversion timeline default:** 6 months for C2H, sometimes 3 or 4. Ask explicitly
- **"Open rate" recruiter speak:** bill rate not yet locked, recruiter wants candidate's number first. Counter by asking for the budgeted range
