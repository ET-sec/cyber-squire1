Business Operations

- Draft proposals, statements of work, master service agreements, and pricing structures
- Negotiate and execute client contracts with full signing authority
- Manage all invoicing, accounts receivable, vendor payments, and bookkeeping
- Oversee tax compliance, regulatory filings, and corporate recordkeeping
- Make all hiring, financial, infrastructure, and operational decisions independently
