# Amex HM — Questions to Ask

Pick 3-4 for the HM screen. Save specific ones for panel rounds if you advance.

---

## Tier 1 — Ask These First

### Q1: "What's the highest-weighted risk on the TRIS roadmap this year?"

**Why it works:** Invites HM to talk about their actual priority. If they say "third-party / supply chain," you know the 2024 breach experience is still shaping the program. If they say "AI adoption," you lean your AI Security background heavier. If they say "cloud migration," you pivot to your Zero Trust architecture work.

### Q2: "How does AppSec engineering interface with the engineering teams building on Go + Istio + GKE?"

**Why it works:** Signals you've done homework on Amex's public tech stack. His answer tells you how tight the feedback loop is between AppSec and development — are you embedded with engineering teams, or separate function reviewing from outside.

### Q3: "What does success look like at the 90-day mark for someone in this contract seat?"

**Why it works:** Gets concrete. His answer reveals whether they expect you to ramp on specific tools, deliver specific artifacts, or build relationships with specific teams. Calibrates what you'd focus on the first two weeks.

### Q4: "The CISO transition to Gleb Reznik happened in October. How has that reset priorities for TRIS Engineering specifically?"

**Why it works:** Signals you researched leadership. Gives the HM an opening to describe strategic context beyond the JD. Shows you think about org-level dynamics.

### Q5: "What's the relationship between TRIS Architecture, Engineering, and Operations pillars day-to-day?"

### Q6: "How is AppSec involved in M&A or new vendor onboarding? Is that scoped in the contract role, or a separate function?"

### Q7: "What does the CRI Profile evidence and audit cadence look like? Quarterly internal reviews, annual external attestation, continuous control monitoring?"

### Q10: "What's the testing and validation workflow when a new SAST or SCA rule gets proposed? Who owns review, who owns deployment, how are false positives managed?"

## Tier 3 — Only If Time Permits

### Q11: "Amex's public engineering posts have referenced WebAssembly for internal FaaS. Does that touch this role, and if so, how do you approach Wasm security today?"

**Technical flex question. Only ask if the HM is technical-deep and conversation has been going well.** Signals you read Amex engineering blog posts.

### Q12: "What are you most proud of in the TRIS program over the last two years?"

**Invitation for HM to share highlight. Builds rapport.** Only ask in the last 2-3 minutes if the call has been positive.

### Q13: "How does Experis — Darren specifically — typically engage with contractors post-placement? Does he stay involved in the relationship or hand off to Amex internally?"

**Logistics question for later. Save for post-offer if it's relevant.**

---

## Questions to NEVER Ask in the HM Screen

- Anything about salary, bonus equivalents, contract rate negotiation. Save for Darren conversations.
- Anything about PTO — contractors typically don't get it; asking signals you didn't read the contract.
- "What's the culture like?" — Glassdoor answers this; ask about specific aspects instead.
- "Is this role stable?" — contract is through 12/31/2026; the JD answers this.
- "Would Amex sponsor a visa?" — not applicable for you.
- "Do you do on-call?" — AppSec rotation is role-specific; ask "how is incident response handled in your pillar?" if you want to know.
- Anything about the 2024 third-party breach by name. Let HM bring it up if they want.
- "What would you change about the program?" — invites HM to share internal critique they'd regret voicing to an external candidate.

---

## The Close-Out Question

At 40-45 minutes, if time permits:

"Is there anything in my background you want me to expand on before we wrap?"

**Why this works:** Invites objection. His answer tells you what you didn't address. If he says "no, we're good" — you likely cleared. If he hesitates or raises something — last chance to address it in the call.
