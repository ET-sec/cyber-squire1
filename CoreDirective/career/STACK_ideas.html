05 Information & alert pipeline eBPF → router → fan-out

Detection happens at the kernel via Falco eBPF. Routing happens at Falcosidekick. Fan-out lands in Datadog (long-term), Telegram (immediate), and Sentry (errors).

Alert routing rules (Falcosidekick)

SeveritySinksSLA
Critical (containerd, CVE-bound)Datadog + Telegram + Sentry + PG< 60 s
High (privilege escalation, unexpected exec)Datadog + Telegram + PG< 5 min
Medium (policy drift, unexpected mount)Datadog + PGbatched 5 min
Info (audit, baseline)Datadog (logs only)batched 15 min
Squire ingestion

Squire pulls from the Datadog alert stream every 5 minutes, scores each alert with the eval harness, and either auto-closes (low risk + matching playbook), enriches with pgvector RAG (medium), or escalates to @CDirective_bot for human review (high). Cuts Datadog triage 80%.
