## 3. The AI Data Specialist Role — Verified Scope

**From Built In Req 10296 (publicly available):**

> "As a NICE AI Data Specialist, you are responsible for supporting and driving the use of AI tool sets to define, develop, manage, and improve NICE Enlighten AI's data solutions. AI Data Specialists are experts in the value and insights from structured data derived from unstructured Customer Experience (CX) interactions, and their technical fluency allows the Enlighten AI data team to build scalable out of the box data structures that drive operational and measurable outcomes."

**What this means in plain terms:**
- Customer service calls, chats, emails are unstructured (voice transcripts, free text, attachments, metadata)
- Enlighten AI needs to turn unstructured interactions into structured data (intent labels, sentiment scores, topic tags, agent quality metrics)
- "Pre-defined data structures" = the schemas, taxonomies, and label systems that classify those interactions
- "Precision and recall" = the ML evaluation metrics that measure how well the data structures capture real intent

**Day-to-day (inferred from scope):**
- Define + refine taxonomies for intent classification across industries (healthcare call center ≠ retail ≠ financial services)
- Validate data structure changes for precision/recall regression
- Test evolving tools from the Research team (likely internal NICE ML research)
- Give feedback to Research + Product on integration with the CEA product family (Customer Engagement Analytics)
- Cross-functional — sits between Research (building the models), Product (owning customer-facing features), and Solution Sales (where this role reports)

**Why this role reports to VP Solution Sales (not VP Engineering):**
- NICE's CX bets get sold to enterprise customers
- Solution Sales needs to confidently articulate "how good is Enlighten AI, where are the evaluation thresholds, what edge cases does it handle" — all of which require clean precision/recall evidence
- The AI Data Specialist is the technical fluency behind the pitch

**What this is NOT:**
- Not pure ML research (that's the Research team)
- Not production ML engineering (that's Engineering)
- Not AI security (your default lens) — unless scope includes security posture of the data pipelines
- Hybrid technical evaluator + internal subject matter expert + customer-facing evidence generator
