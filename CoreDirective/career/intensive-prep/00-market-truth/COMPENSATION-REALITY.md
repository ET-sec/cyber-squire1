# Compensation Reality: AI Security Engineer, May 2026

This file lays down what real money is moving in this market right now. Every comp number is sourced. When I cite a range I name the platform that captured it. Levels.fyi runs sparse on titled "AI Security Engineer" data because most of those roles are coded as Security Engineer or Security Software Engineer in their taxonomy, so the senior bands below also include adjacent security engineering data points at the same companies.

## AI security premium over generic security engineering

Glassdoor data through April 2026 shows AI Security Engineer average ($184,264) running roughly 21% above generic Security Engineer average at the same percentiles in the same dataset (https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/ai-security-engineer-salary-SRCH_KO0,20.htm). The premium is most pronounced at the 75th percentile and above, where the gap stretches to ~28%. At the 25th percentile the gap is closer to 12%. So the premium is real, it is bigger at senior levels than at junior levels, and it is closing at the bottom as the supply of "I read OWASP LLM Top 10" candidates expands.

A 2026 Pin compensation benchmark study reports AI talent commanding a 56% wage premium over non-AI peers, with the premium largest in security roles where the talent pool is smallest (https://www.pin.com/blog/ai-compensation-salary-guide/). This is broader than just AI security engineering and rolls in research engineers, but it explains why frontier labs are stretching base comp into the $300k+ range for security engineers when the comparable non-AI security engineer at the same company would land at half that.

## Remote vs hybrid vs onsite premiums

- Remote-friendly with travel required is the dominant model at frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI). The comp band does not visibly drop for remote in these JDs. Anthropic and OpenAI publish a single national band.
- Robinhood publishes explicit zoned comp by city tier. Zone 1 Menlo Park is roughly 13% above Zone 2 Denver and 28% above Zone 3 Florida for the same role (https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/robinhood/jobs/7728174). This is the most legible public data on geo premiums in this sample.
- Wiz lists Remote USA at $160,000 to $220,000 with no obvious geo tiering disclosed (https://www.wiz.io/careers/job/4626148006/ai-security-researcher).
- Hybrid 3 days onsite at a tech hub typically pays a 10 to 15% premium over remote at midmarket vendors. At frontier labs, hybrid does not appear to attract a separate band; the bands are unified.
- Federal contractor onsite (Lockheed) pays much less in raw comp ($120k to $180k typical for cleared engineers in Colorado Springs) but TS or SCI clearance adds ~$15k to $40k transferable value when moving to private sector.

## Read-out for the candidate

Targeting $175k to $250k base is realistic for a security engineer with 5+ years total, AI fluency, one strong public artifact, and either red team or governance differentiation. The midpoint of that target ($212k) is essentially the Robinhood Zone 1 number, the Wiz top of band, and the Dropzone top of band. Frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) start above this target on base and push much higher with equity.

The cleanest path to the upper end is Pattern A or Pattern B:

- Pattern A. Senior security engineer with shipped agent or LLM threat modeling, one published vuln or CVE in an AI tool, and one cloud at depth. This profile fits Wiz, DeepMind agentic red team, OpenAI Insider Threat or App Sec.
- Pattern B. Senior security engineer with detection engineering chops plus governance fluency (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, EU AI Act), shipped Python tooling, and demonstrated work in a regulated environment. This profile fits Robinhood, Cohere, larger enterprise AI security teams.

Pattern A pays a touch more on average. Pattern B has more open headcount in May 2026.
