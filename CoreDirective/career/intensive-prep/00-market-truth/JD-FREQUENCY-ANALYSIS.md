# JD Frequency Analysis: AI Security Engineer Market, May 2026

Source-grounded analysis of 25 active or recently active AI Security Engineer, AI/ML Security Engineer, LLM Security Engineer, AI Red Team, and adjacent roles. Research window: April 28 to May 8, 2026. Used as ground-truth for QC against the candidate curriculum.

Sample bias note: Frontier-lab roles (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, Mistral) are over-represented at the top of the comp band. Vendor and enterprise roles fill the $140k to $230k middle. Federal contractor roles (Lockheed) require clearance and a different stack.

## How to read this document

For each JD, fields appear verbatim where available. When a JD field could not be confirmed from the source, the field is marked [UNVERIFIED]. The aggregate frequency tier is built only from JDs where the skill list could be confirmed (n=18 confirmed).

## Aggregate skill-frequency tiers (n=18 fully confirmed JDs)

Methodology: Each JD where the explicit required-skills bullet list could be parsed counts once per skill. Synonym normalization: "AWS or GCP or Azure" counts each cloud separately if listed; "Python" rolls up Python, Python production, Python or Rust or Go variants where Python is listed as primary or example. Cert mentions counted separately.

### Certs

Of 18 fully parsed JDs, certifications were required in only 1 (Lockheed Martin, DoD 8570 IAT II minimum). All other JDs explicitly state no certifications required, or do not list any. Industry common certs (CISSP, OSCP, CCSP, CASP+, Sec+) appear as "preferred" in zero of the frontier-lab JDs in this sample.

### Read-out for the candidate

The current $175k to $250k AI Security Engineer interview pulls heavily from Tier A. If a candidate cannot show production Python, at least one cloud at depth, and operational LLM or agent architecture knowledge with a working understanding of prompt injection, the interview will not progress past round two. Tier B is differentiation, not foundation, and most differentiation comes from one of these clusters: red-team and offensive proof, governance and compliance fluency for SOC 2 plus AI Act readiness, or hands-on AI red-team tool fluency (PyRIT, Garak, Promptfoo).
